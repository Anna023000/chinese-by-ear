02Grammar

1. 你 and 您

Nǐ is everyday “you.” Nín is respectful. Use 您好 for a more polite greeting.

2. Plural 们 (men)

Add 们 to pronouns and some words for people: 你 → 你们 (you, plural), 学生 → 学生们 (students), 同学 → 同学们 (classmates). It is not a plural ending for every noun.

3. Tone changes

你好: nǐ hǎo → ní hǎo in speech. When two third tones occur together, the first becomes a rising second tone.

不客气: bù → bú before kè, a fourth tone. Say bú kèqi.
